# Third-party components

The built file `swimlane-editor.html` contains these libraries. They are inlined unchanged by `build.sh` from the `vendor/` folder. Their license and copyright notices stay inside the files.

| svg2pdf.js | 2.2.4 | MIT | (c) 2015-2023 yWorks GmbH, (c) 2013-2015 Vitaly Puzrin | https://github.com/yWorks/svg2pdf.js | `vendor/LICENSE-svg2pdf.js.txt` |

jsPDF's distribution bundles smaller components (for example the fflate compression library and image decoders) under their own permissive licenses. Their notices are kept in `vendor/jspdf.umd.min.js`.
